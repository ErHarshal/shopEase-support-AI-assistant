# ShopEase Shipping Policy

Last updated: January 2026

## Shipping methods and delivery times

We ship across India. Delivery time is counted in business days (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays) from the day your order is shipped.

| Method | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard shipping | 4 to 7 business days | ₹49 |
| Express shipping | 2 to 3 business days | ₹129 |
| Same-day delivery | Same day if ordered before 12 PM | ₹199 |

Same-day delivery is available only in selected cities: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Pune.

## Free shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more, after discounts are applied. Express and same-day delivery are always charged, but express shipping is free for ShopEase Plus members on orders above ₹1,999.

## Order processing time

Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days after payment is confirmed. Orders placed after 6 PM, on Sundays, or on public holidays are processed on the next business day.

Orders placed during sale events may take up to 3 business days to process.

## Order tracking

When your order ships, you receive an email and SMS with a tracking number and a tracking link. You can also track your order from the "My Orders" page in your account.

Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear after the order is shipped.

The order statuses you may see are:

- Processing: we are packing your order.
- Shipped: the courier has picked up your order.
- Out for delivery: your order will arrive today.
- Delivered: the order has been delivered.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled.

## Delivery attempts

The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. If all attempts fail, the order is returned to our warehouse and we will contact you to arrange redelivery or a refund. A refund for an undelivered order excludes the shipping fee if the failure was due to an incorrect address or an unavailable customer.

## Changing your delivery address

You can change the delivery address only before the order is shipped. Contact support as soon as possible with your order number and the new address. Once the order has the status "Shipped", the address cannot be changed.

## Delays

Delivery times are estimates and can be affected by weather, public holidays, strikes, or high order volumes. If your order has not arrived 3 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support with your order number and we will raise a request with the courier.

If an order is lost in transit, we will send a replacement or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## Remote locations

Deliveries to remote areas, islands, and some pin codes in the North East and Jammu and Kashmir may take 3 to 5 extra business days. Same-day and express delivery may not be available for these locations. You can check your pin code on the product page before placing an order.

## International shipping

We currently ship only within India. International shipping is planned for later in the year.

## Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available on orders up to ₹15,000 in most pin codes. A cash on delivery fee of ₹40 applies. Cash on delivery is not available on same-day delivery orders.

## Damaged packages

If the package looks damaged or tampered with at delivery, please refuse it or record a short unboxing video, and contact support within 7 days. See our returns policy for how damaged items are handled.
