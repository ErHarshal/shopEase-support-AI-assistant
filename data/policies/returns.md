# ShopEase Returns and Refunds Policy

Last updated: January 2026

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The 30 days are counted from the day the order status changed to "Delivered", not from the day you placed the order.

Items returned after 30 days are not eligible for a refund or exchange. In exceptional cases, such as a defect that appears later, our support team may review the request individually.

## Eligibility conditions

An item is eligible for return if all of the following are true:

- It is unused, unwashed, and in its original condition.
- It has all original tags, labels, and packaging.
- It includes all accessories, manuals, and free gifts that came with it.
- You have the order number or proof of purchase.

## Items that cannot be returned

The following items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged, unless they arrive damaged or defective:

- Innerwear, socks, and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Personal care and beauty products that have been opened
- Gift cards and digital downloads
- Perishable goods such as food and flowers
- Customised or personalised products
- Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page

## Damaged, defective, or wrong items

If your item arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered:

- Report it within 7 days of delivery.
- Share your order number and clear photos of the item and the packaging.
- We will arrange a free pickup and offer a replacement or a full refund, including the original shipping charge.

Damage or defects reported after 7 days may still be reviewed, but approval is not guaranteed.

## How to start a return

1. Contact support with your order number and the item you want to return.
2. Tell us the reason for the return.
3. Once your request is approved, you will receive a return confirmation with pickup details.
4. Pack the item securely in its original packaging and hand it to the pickup agent.

Do not ship the item back on your own before your return is approved, as we cannot refund returns that were not authorised.

## Return shipping cost

- Returns because of a damaged, defective, or wrong item: free pickup.
- Returns for any other reason (size, change of mind): a return shipping fee of ₹79 is deducted from your refund.
- Free pickup is not available in all pin codes. If pickup is not available, we will share instructions for sending the item by courier and reimburse the courier cost for damaged, defective, or wrong items.

## Refund approval

Refund requests are reviewed by our support team before they are processed.

- Refunds up to ₹5,000 are usually approved within 1 business day if the item meets the eligibility conditions.
- Refunds above ₹5,000 require manual approval by a senior support agent and may take up to 2 business days.
- Any refund request always needs a human approval before money is returned. The assistant can prepare the request, but it cannot approve it.

## Refund timeline and method

Refunds are issued after the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes a quality check, which takes 2 to 3 business days after pickup.

After approval, the refund goes to your original payment method:

| Payment method | Refund time after approval |
|---|---|
| UPI | 2 to 4 business days |
| Credit or debit card | 5 to 7 business days |
| Net banking | 5 to 7 business days |
| ShopEase wallet | Instant |
| Cash on delivery | Bank transfer in 5 to 7 business days, or wallet credit instantly |

For cash on delivery orders, we will ask for your bank account or UPI details to process the refund.

## Exchanges

You can exchange an item for a different size or colour within 30 days of delivery, subject to stock availability. If the new item is not in stock, we will refund you instead. Exchanges follow the same eligibility conditions as returns. The first exchange for a size issue is free.

## Partial refunds

We may issue a partial refund if:

- The item shows signs of use or wear that go beyond trying it on.
- The item is returned without some of its original accessories or packaging.
- Part of a bundle or combo is returned (the discount applied to the bundle will be adjusted).

## Cancelled orders

If you cancel an order before it is shipped, you get a full refund to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. See the FAQ for how to cancel an order.
